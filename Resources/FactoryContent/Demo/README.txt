LumenDSP demo DI clips
======================

Synthetic dry guitar-like signals for auditioning presets without a live instrument.

- Glass_DI_Riff.wav  — open arpeggio for clean/glass tones
- Lead_DI_Line.wav   — higher line for lead sustain checks
- Tone_Sweep.wav     — log sweep for quick cab/EQ response

These files are original to LumenDSP (generated procedurally) and are covered by the project MIT license.
